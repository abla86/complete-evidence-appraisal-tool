# Evidence Appraisal Suite — launch offer

## One-sentence offer
A structured workspace for critical appraisal and evidence traceability that helps researchers document what they decided, why they decided it, and where the supporting evidence is located.

## Target users
Primary:
- master students working with evidence-based practice;
- PhD/research students;
- researchers conducting evidence reviews;
- review teams.

Secondary:
- educators teaching evidence-based practice;
- quality-improvement teams;
- methodology support units.

## Problem
Critical appraisal work is often spread across PDFs, spreadsheets, notes and reference managers. This makes provenance and reviewer comparison harder to maintain.

## Product promise
The software should make the workflow more structured and auditable without pretending that an algorithm can replace methodological judgement.

## Launch CTA
Start free → complete one appraisal → export the result → decide whether Pro saves enough time to justify payment.

## Commercial hypothesis
Users who complete one appraisal with the free workspace will pay for extended workflow, traceability and reviewer features if these reduce repeated manual work.

This is a hypothesis, not a validated market finding.

## Do not promise
Never market the product as:
- a replacement for researchers;
- a clinical decision system;
- a certification of evidence quality;
- an automatic peer reviewer;
- a guarantee of publication;
- a guarantee that an AI-generated assessment is correct.

## Proof assets
1. live demo;
2. GitHub repository;
3. methodology source register;
4. transparent limitations;
5. reproducible examples;
6. short screen-recorded demonstrations;
7. downloadable starter workflow.

## First 10-customer experiment
Recruit 10 relevant users. Give each free access and one realistic appraisal workflow. Then offer the paid version.

Record completion rate, time saved, requested features, willingness to pay, objections and conversion.

Do not fabricate testimonials, user numbers or revenue claims.
