# Research Safety and Verification

Evidence Appraisal Tool is an evidence-extraction and critical-appraisal support tool. Automated findings are candidates for researcher verification, not final scientific judgements.

## Mandatory safety rules

- Not found is never equivalent to No.
- Uncertain findings remain uncertain until a researcher verifies them.
- The system must never invent evidence, page numbers, DOI, authors, methods, or source locations.
- Every automated finding must retain its source location where reliably available.
- Researchers can manually add evidence when the automated analysis cannot find or interpret relevant information.
- Manually added evidence is explicitly labelled and retained in the audit trail.
- Final judgements require researcher review; multi-reviewer workflows must preserve disagreements and consensus decisions.
- Document hashes identify the exact document version used for an appraisal.
- Instrument-specific rules must not be replaced by a generic score.

## Instrument safeguards

### AMSTAR 2
AMSTAR 2 is used as a structured critical-appraisal instrument. The application must not reduce AMSTAR 2 to a numerical total score.

### CASP
CASP checklists are design-specific. The reviewer must select or confirm the checklist appropriate to the study design.

### AGREE II
AGREE II uses 23 items across six domains and a seven-point response scale. Domain scores must follow the AGREE II calculation method.

### GRADE
GRADE is outcome/evidence-base oriented. It must not be presented as a generic quality score for a single article. Certainty may remain undetermined when the evidence base is insufficient.

## Completion gate

An assessment must not be marked complete when required evidence, rationale, reviewer verification, unresolved disagreements, document identity, or mandatory instrument items remain unresolved.

## Scope

The application supports critical appraisal and evidence documentation. It is not itself a validated measurement instrument, and its automated extraction/classification functions require human verification before research conclusions are made.
