Built for evidence work — not AI hype

The project separates deterministic methodology rules from optional AI assistance and keeps human verification in the workflow. AI-generated suggestions should be treated as untrusted assistance and checked against authoritative sources before use in academic, clinical or other high-stakes contexts.
