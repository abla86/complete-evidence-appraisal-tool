# SKILL.md

## Purpose
Metascientific evidence-appraisal platform for critical appraisal, methodology validation and research integrity.

## Skills demonstrated
- Research-method and appraisal workflow modelling
- JBI, AMSTAR 2, AGREE II, CASP, RoB 2, GRADE/CERQual and related instrument handling
- Version/source traceability
- Evidence-location and rationale capture
- Dual-review and inter-rater workflow concepts
- Deterministic assessment engines
- Unit, integration and reference-validation testing
- AI-assistance boundaries and academic-integrity controls

## Methodological rule
Do not invent scores, cut-offs or interpretation models that the authoritative instrument does not support. Distinguish missing evidence from a negative finding and distinguish AI candidates from researcher-confirmed evidence.

## Maintenance
Methodology sources, versions, licensing/usage statements, assessment engines and contract tests must be kept synchronized.
